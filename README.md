> ### *I learned this job by shipping it — not by studying it.*

Graphic designer and creative director from Mongolia 🇲🇳 with five-plus years of shipped brand work for startups: identity, posters, key art and the campaign systems a launch actually ships with. Lately I build too, by describing the thing to AI and steering it until it looks like someone meant it.

<img src="https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=rect&height=3&color=d6ff32" width="100%" />

## ✦ What I do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Graphic design** | Posters, key art, marks and the visual world a product lives in. The thing people screenshot. |
| **Marketing design** | Launch kits, social systems, landing narratives, KOL packs. Creative that has to perform in a four-week window. |
| **Creative direction** | Story, positioning, partnerships and craft, aligned so the brand doesn't look like one thing and sell like another. |
| **Pixel & character** | Sprites, portraits, collection identity. Hands on in Aseprite, not just directed. |
| **Launch systems** | Whitepapers, Gitbook, decks and the visual kit a drop actually ships with. |
| **Partnerships** | Deals, grants and co-marketing that sit next to the pictures. |

## ✦ How I work

```text
01  ADOPT     the brief walks in messy. I take it as it is.
02  LEARN     I sit in the mechanic until I can draw it.
03  SHIFT     if it's alive and it works, we redraw, then we spend.
04  DEVELOP   marks, surfaces, the kit a launch actually ships with.
05  VIBE      describe it to AI, squint like a designer, ship it.   ← new
```
